Benchmarks

The recorded results of the benchmark suite in benches/runtime, with the commit, the machine and the store's durability mode. They are the baseline the regression gate compares against. Later M18 phases add their before and after figures here.

Running the suite

cargo bench -p vertep-bench
python3 scripts/bench-gate.py collect --out results.json

VERTEP_BENCH_QUICK=1 skips the 100,000-entry activation case. The CI job bench-gate runs the suite at the last release tag and at the change, on one runner, and fails when a case is slower than its threshold (25% unless benches/baseline.json says otherwise). A baseline moves only with a recorded reason: scripts/bench-gate.py record --results results.json --reason "...", then scripts/bench-gate.py page rewrites this page.

Budgets that count, not time

Wall-clock figures depend on the machine. The budgets the runtime promises are counts of store calls instead, checked by the tests in crates/vertep-runtime/tests/budgets.rs through CountingStorage: a turn commits once, activation reads only the entries after the latest snapshot, and a duplicate message is dropped without reading the journal from the start. The runtime does not meet them yet, so those tests are ignored until M18 phase 2. Today a turn with one send makes five commits, and activation reads the whole journal even when a snapshot is ten entries from the head.
