A theatre in a box

The vertep is the travelling puppet theatre of Ukrainian Christmas: a wooden box of two tiers, carried from house to house behind a star on a pole, with the goat dancing among the carollers. Its puppets cannot wander. Each moves along a slot cut into the stage floor, and the puppeteer, however clever, can only choose which slot a puppet takes next.

Vertep the library is built the same way. A chart is the stage with its slots. An actor is a puppet that moves only along transitions. A guard, a person or a language model may choose the next move, but none of them can cut a new slot. And the whole play is written down, so it can be performed again, exactly, without the puppeteer.

The way of one event

Nothing nondeterministic touches the core. Time, randomness, model output and the network arrive only as events in a mailbox, and each one is written to the journal before the pure step function sees it.

step takes a configuration, a datamodel and an event, and returns the next configuration and a list of effects. The shell carries out the effects: it sends messages, arms timers and asks deciders. Their answers come back as new events.

So the journal is the whole truth of an actor. Replay it and you get the same states, on a server, in a browser or in the Lean model.

A choice point, in the chart's own words

From crates/vertep-agent/examples/support-agent.scxml: the model may call a tool, finish or escalate, and nothing else.

<state id="decide" vt:decide="llm/claude" vt:fallback="human/support" vt:deadline="30s"
       vt:maxDecisions="10" vt:candidates="call_tool finish escalate">
  <transition event="call_tool" target="tooling">
    <send event="tool.call" targetexpr="ev.data.tool"><content expr="ev.data.args"/></send>
  </transition>
  <transition event="finish" target="done"><assign location="answer" expr="ev.data"/></transition>
  <transition event="escalate" target="human"/>
  <transition event="vt.agent.budget-exhausted" target="budget_out"/>
  <transition event="error.decide.invalid" target="decide"/>
  <transition event="vt.decide.refused" target="human"/>
</state>

On entry, the core computes the events that would be enabled and offers them to the decider named llm/claude. Its answer returns as the event vt.decision, is journaled, and is accepted only if it was one of the offered candidates. If no answer comes within 30 seconds, the durable deadline hands the choice to a person. After ten decisions, the budget event ends the loop.
